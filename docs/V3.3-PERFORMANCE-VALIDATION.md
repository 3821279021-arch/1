## Capability-mode boundary

`Unrestricted` removes or raises **soft** constraints such as history slicing, response length, forced concise speech and forced speech. It does **not** bypass:

- seat-scoped information visibility;
- legal game action validation;
- credential / SSRF protections;
- operator request/token/cost budgets;
- transport safety limits.

For self-hosted maximum-capability experiments, operators should explicitly size `AI_GAME_TOKEN_BUDGET`, `AI_DAILY_TOKEN_BUDGET` and any `AI_PROMPT_TOKEN_LIMIT` to the intended cost envelope.
