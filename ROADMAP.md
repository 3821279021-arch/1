# Roadmap

Current: V3.1 environment reproducibility, research export, CLI batches and basic statistical comparisons. Public licensing and asset provenance need owner decisions before publishing.

Next (V3.2): round-robin/Swiss, role/faction-aware ratings, language-quality judging with blind human review, and stronger paired statistical inference. ELO is supplementary to sample counts and detailed metrics.

Later (V4): external Agent protocol, Python/TypeScript SDKs and authorized spectator streams. Reuse the existing observation boundary and authoritative rules.

Non-goals: rewrite the frontend/backend, add unrelated payment/marketing features, collect provider hidden reasoning, or claim bit-for-bit cloud-model reproducibility.
