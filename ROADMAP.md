# Roadmap

Current: V3.2.0 play experience, unattended-room suspension, local atmosphere audio, model preferences, app shell and constrained casual boards; V3.1 reproducibility, export and batches remain supported. Public licensing and asset provenance need owner decisions before publishing.

Next (V3.3 Tournament & Evaluation): round-robin/Swiss, role/faction-aware ratings, language-quality judging with blind human review, and stronger paired statistical inference. ELO is supplementary to sample counts and detailed metrics.

Later (V4): external Agent protocol, Python/TypeScript SDKs and authorized spectator streams. Reuse the existing observation boundary and authoritative rules.

Non-goals: rewrite the frontend/backend, add unrelated payment/marketing features, collect provider hidden reasoning, or claim bit-for-bit cloud-model reproducibility.
